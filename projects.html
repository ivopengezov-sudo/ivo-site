PaletPlaner

Packing irregular sheet-metal parts onto pallets for shipping used to be a manual, eyeball-it decision — often wasting pallet space. PaletPlaner reads the real part shapes from uploaded DXF files and automatically computes the optimal multi-pallet layout, respecting real weight limits and stacking height. Designed and developed entirely independently, in real daily use — now expanding toward a subscription tool for other machine shops.

Machining Time — CNC Calculator

Estimating machining time from a technical drawing is usually a manual, experience-based guess — easy to get wrong on parts combining drilling, milling and tapping. This tool reads a PDF drawing with AI — recognizing holes, threads, contours and tolerances — and feeds the data straight into a calculator that computes real machining time using the shop's own tools, materials and machines, not generic defaults. Fully bilingual interface (Bulgarian/English).

Kamenna Stryaha — Guesthouse Demo Site

Small family-run guesthouses often have no site of their own, relying only on a Booking.com listing — with no story about the place, the rooms, or the kitchen to convince a visitor to book directly. A demo site for a guesthouse in the Rhodope Mountains (built 1912, three rooms, Rhodope cuisine) — presenting the rooms, a date picker for booking inquiries, and the location. Built to show what a proper online presence looks like for a small family hospitality business.

WooCommerce Discount Plugin

Manually creating and updating coupon codes for volume discounts is easy to forget and easy to get wrong. This plugin removes that step entirely — once a customer's cart subtotal crosses a configurable threshold, a 10% discount is applied automatically as a cart fee, no coupon code needed. Built with WooCommerce's native cart hook (woocommerce_cart_calculate_fees) and tested on a live WooCommerce store.

WordPress Testimonials Plugin

Client testimonials scattered across emails, screenshots, and a generic contact-form plugin are hard to keep organized and even harder to display consistently. This plugin gives testimonials their own structured content type — author name, role, and star rating — rendered through a simple shortcode into a responsive grid, plus a REST endpoint for pulling the same data into a headless frontend.
